Launch Project Idea

The Burden of
Small Choices

Many people face decision fatigue from having to make multiple small choices every single day.

Even simple decisions can feel stressful and time-consuming, especially when there is no clear best option. This leads to unnecessary delays in your workflow and daily routine.

Meet Our User

The Overthinking Student

Demographic: Age 18 - 20, Student or Young Adult living in Thailand.

Tech Skill: Normal/Average.

Daily Problem: They spend too much time thinking about small decisions (like what to eat, what to choose, or what to do next), which causes stress and delays simple actions.

Why Existing Apps Aren't Enough

  • Asking Friends Takes Time Waiting for someone else to make a decision for you is inefficient and doesn't always provide a helpful or immediate answer.
  • Random Methods Feel Unreliable Using a coin flip or random wheel feels disconnected from reality and often leaves you doubting the outcome.
  • Existing Apps Are Too Complex Most tools on the market are built for heavy productivity, making them too bloated for simple daily choices.

The DecisionLite Solution

Simplicity at its Core

DecisionLite allows users to quickly enter their options and receive a simple suggestion to help them make a decision without overthinking.

It's designed to be the fastest route between hesitation and action, wrapped in a clean, distraction-free interface.

Core Features (MVP)

Option Input

Users add choices easily. This acts as the frictionless starting point for any decision.

One-Tap Decision

Suggests an option instantly. Significantly reduces thinking time and mental pressure.

Decision History

Saves past results. Perfect for reflecting on choices or quickly pulling up routine decisions.

Metrics for Success

100%
Focus on Time Saved

Faster Decisions, Fewer Steps

Our primary success metric is the speed at which a user can go from confusion to a decided action.

If users can make decisions faster with fewer steps compared to their traditional methods, DecisionLite is considered highly successful.

Risks & Feasibility

The UI Challenge

Hardest Part: Designing a simple interface that feels helpful without being overwhelming. We will mitigate this using Figma AI for rapid UI/UX iterations.

8–10 Week Plan

Feasibility: Yes. I am confident the app can be completed within 8-10 weeks. The scope focuses purely on a small set of core features, allowing enough time for prototyping and iteration using AI assistants.
